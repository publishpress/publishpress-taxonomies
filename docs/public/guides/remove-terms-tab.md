# Remove terms

Table of Contents

- [Remove terms from all posts](#0-toc-title)
- [Remove terms to posts with specific terms](#1-toc-title)

Taxonomies has a feature that allows you to quickly remove terms from your content.

You will find this feature on the “[Manage Terms](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-to-manage-terms/)” screen in Taxonomies. This feature works for any WordPress taxonomy.

## Remove terms from all posts [#](#0-toc-title)

This feature allows you to remove a term from every post on your site. In the image below, I’m removing the term “security” from every post.

![Remove terms from posts](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/security-remove-term-1024x491.png)

*security remove term*

- Click “Remove”.
- You’ll see a message confirming that the term was added to all posts:

![Remove terms message](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/manage-terms-remove-1024x136.png)

*manage terms remove*

## Remove terms to posts with specific terms [#](#1-toc-title)

The “Remove terms” feature also allows you to add a term to only a post that already has a specific term. For example, you could automatically remove “Vegetables” as a term from any post that already has “Carrots”, “Spinach” or “Cabbages” as a term.

- In the image below, I’m removing the term “Food” from any post which already has the terms “Carrots”.
- Click “Add”.

![Remove terms box](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/remove-terms-match-1024x596.png)
