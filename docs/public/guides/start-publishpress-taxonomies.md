# Getting Started with PublishPress Taxonomies

[PublishPress Taxonomies](https://publishpress.com/links/taxonomies) allows you to create and manage Tags, Categories, and all your WordPress taxonomy terms. [PublishPress Taxonomies](https://publishpress.com/links/taxonomies) can help with all your WordPress content, from adding Tags and Categories to WooCommerce Product Categories.

Here are 10 key features in PublishPress Taxonomies:

1. **Manage Terms and Taxonomies**: You can create new taxonomies and edit all the settings for each taxonomy: [Introduction to the Taxonomies Screen](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-taxonomies-screen/).
2. **Terms Display**: This feature allows you to create a customizable display of all the terms in one taxonomy: [Introduction to Terms Display](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-to-tag-clouds/).
3. **Terms for Current Post**: This enables you to create a customizable display of all the terms assigned to the current post: [Introduction to Terms for Current Post](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-to-tags-for-current-post/).
4. **Related Posts**: Shows lists of posts with similar tags and categories: [Introduction to Related Posts](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-to-related-posts/).
5. **Auto Links**: If you have a term called “WordPress”, the Auto Links feature will find any instances of “WordPress” in your content and add links to the archive page for that tag: [Introduction to Auto Links](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-to-auto-links/).
6. **Auto Terms**: If you have a term called “WordPress”, Auto Terms can analyze your posts, and when it finds the word “WordPress”, it can add that term to your post. [Introduction to Auto Terms](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-to-auto-terms/).
7. **Add Terms with AI**: The Pro version of PublishPress Taxonomies has AI integrations with OpenAI, IBM Watson, and more. This makes it easier for you to find or create the best taxonomy terms for your content. [Automatically Add New Terms (Pro)](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/automatically-add-new-terms/).
8. **Term Synonyms**: This feature allows you to have multiple words associated with a single term. [Introduction to Term Synonyms (Pro)](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/synonyms/)
9. **Linked Terms**: You can connect terms. When the main term is added to a post, the linked terms will be added also. [Introduction to Linked Terms (Pro)](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/linked-terms/)
10. **Manage Terms**: Add, rename, merge, remove, delete and even delete unused terms. [Introduction to Manage Terms](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-to-manage-terms/).
