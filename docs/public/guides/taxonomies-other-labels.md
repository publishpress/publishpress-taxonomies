# Taxonomies: Other Labels

This guide will show you how to change the “Other Labels” options for your taxonomies. You can find this by visiting [the Taxonomies screen](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-taxonomies-screen/) and editing a taxonomy.

There are multiple different places in the WordPress admin area where your taxonomy name may appear. You can customize all those labels on this screen. When you first create a new taxonomy in TaxoPress, we will do our best to fill this in automatically.
