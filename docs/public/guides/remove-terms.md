# Remove Terms From All Posts

Taxonomies has a feature that allows you to remove terms from all posts. Unlike the “[Delete terms](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/delete-terms/)” option, the terms will still exist on your site after the removal.

You will find this feature on the “Terms” screen in Taxonomies.

In the example below, I’m removing the term “WordPress Plugins” from any associated posts.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-taxonomies/remove-all-posts-bf3b3481a4.webp)

- Click “Remove From All Posts”.
- You will now see a message showing that the terms were removed.

![remove terms from all posts](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-taxonomies/removed-all-posts-5c72d4141e.webp)
