# Introduction to Related Posts

Table of Contents

- [Example of Related Posts](#example-of-terms-display)

- [Editing Related Posts](#editing-term-displays)

The Related Posts feature in [PublishPress Taxonomies](https://publishpress.com/links/taxonomies) allows you to show content that is related to the current post.

This feature works by checking for shared taxonomy terms. So if your post has the terms “Vegetables” and “Food”, then this feature will likely display other posts that also have the terms “Vegetables” and “Food”. The more terms that are shared, the more likely a post is to show in the “Related Posts” feature.

## Edit or Add New Related Terms [#](#edit-or-add-new-term-displays)

On the “Related Posts” screen, you’ll see all your displays. You can click the title or the “Edit” link to edit the settings for this existing display.

![Edit or Add New Related Terms](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-taxonomies/related-posts-screen-1024x423-1-9b4283011a.webp)

This feature requires the Pro version of the Taxonomies plugin

On the “Related Posts” screen in the Pro version of Taxonomies Pro, you can click “Add New” and add your own display.
