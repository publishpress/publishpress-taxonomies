# Copy Terms

Table of Contents

- [How to Copy Terms](#0-toc-title)
- [Advanced Copy for Tags and Categories](#1-toc-title)

WordPress does not have the ability to copy taxonomy terms. However, that is possible with the Taxonomies plugin.

## How to Copy Terms [#](#0-toc-title)

After installing the Taxonomies plugin, go to the “[Terms](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/terms-screen/)” screen. You can click the “Copy” link next to any term. You will get a copy of the term that is attached to the same posts and taxonomy as the original term.

![Copy a term in WordPress](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-taxonomies/copy-terms-2-6f8160cfb0.webp)

*copy terms*

In the screenshot below you can see there is new term called “open source Copy”. It is attached to the same two posts as the original term. You can now rename that term and remove the “Copy” portion of the name.

![Copied Tag or Category](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/copied-terms-1024x302.png)

*copied terms*

It’s also possible to the term with any metadata that is attached to the term. This will bring over any metadata from plugins that add SEO data, fields, or other extra information to your terms.

![Copy term with metadata](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-taxonomies/copy-with-metadata-6f8160cfb0.webp)

*copy with metadata*

## Advanced Copy for Tags and Categories [#](#1-toc-title)

There is also a more advanced “Copy” feature available. If you choose “Copy” from the “Bulk actions” dropdown, you’ll be able to move the copy of your term to a new taxonomy.

![Advanced copy option for Tags and Categories](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/advanced-copy-term-1024x243.png)

*advanced copy term*

Once you’ve selected the post, you’ll be able to choose a “Destination Taxonomy” from a dropdown. That option is shown in this screenshot below:

![Advanced copy for Tags and Categories](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/advanced-copy-terms-1024x262.png)

*advanced copy terms*

You will now see a copy of the term that is now attached to your new taxonomy. The term will not be attached to any posts.

![Advanced copy completed for Tags and Categories](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/copied-terms-1-1024x298.png)

*copied terms*
