# Merge Terms

Taxonomies has a feature that allows you to merge terms together. You will find this feature on the “[Manage Terms](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-to-manage-terms/)” screen in Taxonomies.

Taxonomies can automatically merge Terms that exist on your site. This is very useful for cleaning up Terms that were created by mistake.

In the image below, I have three Terms that are typos: “Blloggingg”, “Bloggingg”, and “Bloging”. I want to merge them all into the “Blogging” term.

The Terms you want to merge must already exist and be added to content on your site.

![Merge terms](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/merge-terms-1024x656.png)

*merge terms*

Click the “Rename” button and you’ll see a message like the screenshot below.

![Merge terms message in WordPress](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/manage-terms-edited-1024x256.png)

If you’re unsure what terms need merging, try the “Suggest Terms to Merge” button. This will automatically search for and find similar terms.

![Suggest terms to merge](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/suggest-terms-to-merge-1024x424.png)
