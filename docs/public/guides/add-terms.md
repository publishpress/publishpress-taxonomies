# Add Terms

Table of Contents

- [Add terms to all posts](#0-toc-title)
- [Add terms to posts with specific terms](#1-toc-title)

Taxonomies has a feature that allows you to quickly add terms to your content.

You will find this feature on the “[Manage Terms](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/introduction-to-manage-terms/)” screen in Taxonomies. This feature works for any WordPress taxonomy.

## Add terms to all posts [#](#0-toc-title)

This feature allows you to add a term to every post on your site. In the image below, I’m adding the term “Tomato” to every post.

![Add terms to all posts](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/add-tomato-1024x489.png)

- Click “Add”.
- You’ll see a message confirming that the term was added to all posts:

![Terms added message](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/add-terms-success.png)

## Add terms to posts with specific terms [#](#1-toc-title)

The “Add terms” feature also allows you to add a term to only a post that already has a specific term. For example, you could automatically add “Vegetables” as a term to any post that already has “Carrot”, “Spinach” or “Cabbage” as a term.

- In the image below, I’m adding the term “Carrots” to any post which already has the terms “Potatoes” or “Cabbage”.
- Click “Add”.

![Add terms to posts](https://taxopress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/add-terms-match-1024x593.png)
